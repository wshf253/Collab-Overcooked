NAME:
Mashed Potato and Carrot Mixture

INGREDIENTS:
potato (1)
carrot(1)

COOKING STEPs:
1. Cut a potato into slices.
2. Place the potato slices in a pot and cook for 3 timesteps.
3. Put the boiled potato slices and a carrot in a blender and stir for 3 timesteps.
4. Fill a dish with the mixture from the blender and serve.
